# Place Cards — 18 Places

*Created by Claude Opus (via the user's Claude Pro, project "Claude Harness Audit"), 2026-11-13; transcribed from the sheet "Place Cards for Memory Places (18)" (revision 6). Each card has 7 items — ① type, ② key feature, ③ facts that differ from the average image, ④ angle, ⑤ breakable elements and how to handle them, ⑥ crowd density, ⑦ action restrictions — plus the best time of day and why, and English prompt variables (SPOT_DESCRIPTION / KEY_FEATURE / FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE / ANGLE_AND_CROP / PEOPLE / AVOID). Points marked as unverified are Opus's own reservations, preserved from the original.*

*Distribution: type B (recognizable by a part) — Senso-ji, Fushimi Inari, Kiyomizu-dera. Type C (the shape itself) — Miyajima only. Everything else is type A (atmosphere/experience). At Meiji Jingu, only the torii is treated as C; at Don Quijote, only the logo and mascot are treated as D. Best times of day: night 9 cards, morning 6, midday 3.*

*Operating notes (Opus): do not make night postcards of Meiji Jingu (its gates open at sunrise and close at sunset, so the grounds do not exist at night). At an onsen, draw no people in the bath at all (only an empty open-air bath or a foot bath). Distinguish the residential back street at night from the alley with a vending machine by their key features (lit windows / the vending machine's light).*

## 1. Shibuya Scramble Crossing

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience
- Best time of day: night (the smear of neon and rain hides sign text and crowd faces, and the crowd size looks real)
- ② Key feature: the crowd waiting at the curb for the light, and the surge of people mid-crossing
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: all directions turn green at once, and people also cross diagonally. It is almost never empty. Most people walk briskly and don't stop
- ④ Angle: from the edge of the sidewalk, low at about 1 m, looking diagonally across the crossing. Cut the buildings' upper halves at the top of the frame. Muse near a front corner
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: text on large screens and building signs → cut at the top or reduce to smears of light / crowd faces → motion blur mid-crossing, backs / the zebra crossing's white stripes → diagonal, so the count can't be checked / traffic lights → out of focus
- ⑥ Crowd density: very high (a few people in the foreground, a surge of people in the back)
- ⑦ Action restrictions: avoid standing still or posing in the middle of the crosswalk (it reads as dangerous and obstructive). Don't make the Hachiko statue the main subject
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: the edge of the sidewalk at Shibuya Scramble Crossing, Shibuya, Tokyo, waiting for the light
- KEY_FEATURE: a dense crowd surging across the wide zebra crossing in several directions at once, motion-blurred
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: crowds cross diagonally as well as straight; the crossing is never empty; most people walk briskly; tall buildings with large video screens surround the crossing
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: low angle from about 1 m at the curb, the crossing seen at a slant; buildings cut off at the top of the frame; screens appear only as smeared colored light
- PEOPLE: many people, mostly backs and motion-blurred figures; no sharp faces
- AVOID: no readable signs or screen content, no character standing in the middle of the crosswalk, no empty crossing

## 2. Meiji Jingu

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience (the torii is C)
- Best time of day: morning (the gates open at sunrise and close at sunset, so the grounds do not exist at night; morning gravel and dappled light look the most real)
- ② Key feature: the base of the plain-wood great torii pillar, and the wide gravel approach through the forest
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: the torii is unpainted plain wood (Taiwan cypress), not vermilion. The approach is wide gravel. The forest is mostly evergreen trees. At night the shrine is closed and cannot be entered
- ④ Angle: diagonally from the edge of the approach, looking up from a low position. Only one pillar of the torii; cut the kasagi crossbeam at the top of the frame. Muse in a diagonal back view, facing down the approach
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: the structure and crest at the top of the torii → out of frame / the worship hall and main hall → don't use them (if used, only the edge of the copper roof) / text on the sake barrels and ema → diagonal, out of focus
- ⑥ Crowd density: low to medium (a few people, small and blurred, far down the approach)
- ⑦ Action restrictions: no peace signs and no eating or drinking in front of the worship hall. Don't have Muse drink water at the purification fountain. Don't show the inside of the worship hall, or the faces of priests and wedding processions. Don't make night postcards
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: the wide gravel approach path inside the forest of Meiji Jingu shrine, Shibuya, Tokyo
- KEY_FEATURE: the base of a massive unpainted natural-wood torii pillar beside the gravel path
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: the torii is plain weathered grey-brown cypress, NOT vermilion, NOT red; the path is wide and covered in fine gravel; tall dense evergreen trees on both sides
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: from the edge of the path at about 1 m, looking slightly up; only one pillar visible at the frame edge; the top of the gate cut off by the frame; character seen from a three-quarter back angle facing down the path
- PEOPLE: a few visitors far down the path, small and out of focus, walking away
- AVOID: no red or vermilion torii, no shrine building facade, no character eating or making a peace sign, no night scene

## 3. Takeshita Street

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience
- Best time of day: midday (the memory of Takeshita Street is the daytime crowd and the shops' colors; at night the shops start closing and it looks like a different place)
- ② Key feature: the flower-motif entrance arch, the interlocking brick-pattern paving, and the dense stream of people between loud pink shopfronts (a crepe stand's display case) *(revised 2026-11-13 from the owner's real photos — field notes, correction 10; the first version of this card excluded the arch, which was wrong)*
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: the street is paved in interlocking brick-pattern blocks, not asphalt. It slopes gently down from the Harajuku Station side. The entrance arch carries a flower motif, the "Takeshita Street" sign, and seasonal banners. Signage leans pink, large, and dense. Even in the morning there is already a crowd of young visitors — a quiet, empty Takeshita Street is off-key
- ④ Angle: from under or just inside the entrance arch, looking down the slope into the crowd, seen diagonally. Muse standing among the crowd
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: long text and small print → distance, blur / garbled pseudo-text anywhere → reject the shot / character merchandise → don't show it recognizably / text on a crepe wrapper → hide it with the hand's orientation. Short, correctly rendered signs — including the arch's own sign — may stay readable
- ⑥ Crowd density: high (to the point that passers-by's shoulders and arms enter the frame edges)
- ⑦ Action restrictions: avoid eating while walking; stand still in front of a shop holding the crepe. Don't show existing characters' merchandise in a recognizable form
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: under the flower-motif entrance arch of Takeshita Street, looking down the crowded shopping lane, Harajuku, Tokyo
- KEY_FEATURE: the entrance arch overhead, interlocking brick-pattern paving underfoot, and a dense stream of young shoppers between loud pink shopfronts, a crepe stand's display case among them
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: the paving is brick-pattern blocks, not asphalt; the street slopes gently downhill; signage is pink, large and dense; a crowd of young visitors is present even in the morning
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: from within the crowd at about 1 m, slanted view down the slope; the entrance arch visible at the top of the frame; shop signs dense on both sides
- PEOPLE: crowded; shoulders and arms of passers-by cut by the frame edges; faces blurred or turned away
- AVOID: no asphalt ground, no empty or quiet street, no muted small signage, no garbled text, no recognizable character merchandise, no walking while eating

## 4. Senso-ji

- ① Type: B — recognizable by a part
- Best time of day: night (lit up, the lantern and main hall are bright, and there are fewer people; at midday there are too many people and faces break easily)
- ② Key feature: the carved dragon on the underside of Kaminarimon's giant lantern, looking up from directly beneath it
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: the giant lantern is red with large black lettering, and a wooden carved dragon on its underside. In front of the main hall there is a large incense burner, and worshippers bathe in its smoke
- ④ Angle: from directly beneath the giant lantern, looking almost straight up. Muse at the bottom edge of the frame, also looking up. If the main hall is included, show it in the distance, blurred, through the incense burner's smoke
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: the lantern's lettering → not visible from directly below / Nakamise signs → blur / the five-story pagoda → don't use it, or small in the distance / the gate's structure → cut the beams at the frame edges
- ⑥ Crowd density: low to medium at night, very high at midday
- ⑦ Action restrictions: don't show the inside of the main hall. Bathing in the incense smoke with the hands is fine (it is proper practice). Avoid a peace sign in front of the offering box
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: directly beneath the giant red paper lantern of Kaminarimon gate, Asakusa, Tokyo
- KEY_FEATURE: the carved wooden dragon on the underside of the giant lantern, seen from directly below
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: the lantern is huge and red with a carved dragon on its underside; seen from below, its lettering is not visible
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: looking almost straight up from below the lantern; the character at the bottom edge of the frame, also looking up; gate beams cut off by the frame
- PEOPLE: a few people at the frame edges, blurred
- AVOID: no readable lettering on the lantern, no full gate facade, no five-story pagoda in focus

## 5. Miyajima

- ① Type: C — the shape itself
- Best time of day: morning (few people, and deer are on the beach and the approach; in soft light the outline of the torii over the sea breaks less easily)
- ② Key feature: the vermilion great torii standing in the sea (small, in a distant view), and a deer in the foreground
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: the great torii being vermilion is fine (here it matches the average image). It is a ryobu torii, with supporting legs in front of and behind the main pillars. At high tide it floats on the sea; at low tide you can walk across the sand to it. The deer are wild, and feeding them is prohibited
- ④ Angle: from the beach, with a deer and Muse in the foreground and the torii small in the back of the frame, off center. Always specify high tide or low tide
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: the count of the torii's pillars and supporting legs → small in a distant view / the text on the plaque → unreadable at a distance / the repetition of the shrine corridors → don't use them / the deer's antlers and legs → keep focus on Muse
- ⑥ Crowd density: low in the morning, high at midday
- ⑦ Action restrictions: don't have Muse feed or touch the deer (just standing alongside). Don't show ceremonies inside the shrine buildings
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: the beach in front of Itsukushima Shrine, Miyajima island, Hiroshima
- KEY_FEATURE: the vermilion great torii gate standing in the sea in the far background, small in the frame, with a wild deer in the foreground
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: the gate is vermilion with four extra supporting legs, one in front of and one behind each main pillar; {TIDE: at high tide it stands in the water / at low tide it stands on wet sand}; deer roam freely
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: from the beach at about 1 m; the gate small and off-center in the distance; character and deer in the foreground
- PEOPLE: a few distant visitors on the sand, small
- AVOID: no feeding or touching the deer, no close-up of the gate, no shrine corridor, no readable text

## 6. Fushimi Inari

- ① Type: B — recognizable by a part
- Best time of day: morning (few people, and the morning sun shines through the vermilion of the gates; at night there is little lighting and it is dark)
- ② Key feature: inside the tunnel of the Senbon Torii
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: vermilion is fine (it matches the average image). The pillars' bases are black. The gates stand densely, and their size varies by place. The donors' names, in black lettering, are on the back side of the pillars as seen when climbing
- ④ Angle: from inside the tunnel, facing the climbing direction, diagonally. Focus only on Muse; blur the gates more the further they recede
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: the repetition and spacing of the gates → diagonal, blur the distance / lettering on the pillars → not shown when facing the climbing direction / what the fox statues hold in their mouths → don't use, or blur / perfect one-point symmetry → break it slightly with a diagonal
- ⑥ Crowd density: very high near the entrance; low to medium if set partway up the mountain
- ⑦ Action restrictions: don't have Muse lean on, touch, or climb the pillars
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: inside the tunnel of densely packed vermilion torii gates at Fushimi Inari Taisha, Kyoto
- KEY_FEATURE: the gates lined up closely, forming a vermilion tunnel that recedes into the distance
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: the gates are vermilion with black bases; they vary in size; facing uphill, no lettering is visible on the pillars
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: inside the tunnel facing uphill, slightly diagonal; focus only on the character, gates further away increasingly out of focus
- PEOPLE: one or two hikers far ahead in the tunnel, small and blurred
- AVOID: no readable lettering on pillars, no character leaning on or touching the gates, no perfectly symmetrical one-point perspective

## 7. Kiyomizu-dera

- ① Type: B — recognizable by a part
- Best time of day: morning (it opens early and there are few people, and morning light enters the valley's trees; at midday the stage is extremely crowded)
- ② Key feature: the valley's trees and the city of Kyoto, seen over the stage's wooden railing
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: the stage floor is wooden boards, and the railing is also unpainted wood. The Niomon gate and the three-story pagoda are vermilion, but the main hall's stage is not. Autumn maples, spring cherry blossoms
- ④ Angle: on the stage, over the back of Muse standing in front of the railing, looking at the valley and the city. Don't use the standard composition of the whole stage from outside
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: the pillar structure under the stage (the kake-zukuri) → don't use it / the railing's repetition → diagonal, only a few posts / signs in the distant city → unreadable at a distance / the three-story pagoda → don't use it, or in the distance
- ⑥ Crowd density: low to medium in the morning, very high at midday
- ⑦ Action restrictions: don't have Muse climb on the railing or lean out over it
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: on the wooden stage of Kiyomizu-dera temple, Higashiyama, Kyoto, beside the wooden railing
- KEY_FEATURE: the view over the wooden railing into a valley of dense trees with the city of Kyoto beyond
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: the stage floor is broad weathered wooden planks; the railing is plain unpainted wood, not vermilion; {SEASON: autumn maple colors / spring cherry blossoms / summer green}
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: behind the character at the railing, slightly to one side; only a few railing posts visible, at a slant; no view of the stage from outside
- PEOPLE: a few visitors along the railing at the frame edges, blurred
- AVOID: no famous exterior view of the stage on its pillars, no character climbing or leaning over the railing

## 8. Arashiyama Bamboo Grove

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience
- Best time of day: morning (early morning is the only time with few people; thin light falls from above, which brings out the bamboo-grove feel)
- ② Key feature: the path with tall bamboo rising on both sides
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: low brushwood fences line both sides of the path, and you cannot enter the grove. The bamboo is straight and tall, the leaves close overhead, and light comes through in thin patches. At midday it is very crowded, and rickshaws also pass
- ④ Angle: from the edge of the path, with Muse toward the path's edge, looking slightly up at the bamboo. Cut the tops of the bamboo at the top of the frame
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: the bamboo's repetition → focus on Muse, blur the distance / the brushwood fence's mesh → diagonal, blur / graffiti on the bamboo → don't draw it / a symmetrical straight path → shift Muse to the edge to break it
- ⑥ Crowd density: low in the morning (one or two people far away), very high at midday
- ⑦ Action restrictions: don't have Muse touch the bamboo, cross the fence, or write anything on the bamboo (a place where damaging the bamboo has been a problem)
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: the path through the Sagano bamboo grove, Arashiyama, Kyoto
- KEY_FEATURE: very tall straight green bamboo stalks rising on both sides of a narrow path
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: low brushwood fences line both sides of the path and visitors stay on the path; the canopy closes overhead and daylight comes through in thin patches
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: from the edge of the path at about 1 m, looking slightly up; tops of the bamboo cut by the frame; character off-center at the path edge
- PEOPLE: one or two people far down the path, small and blurred
- AVOID: no character inside the grove or touching bamboo, no writing on bamboo, no perfectly symmetrical composition

## 9. Omoide Yokocho

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience
- Best time of day: night (the lanterns are lit and the green entrance gate becomes the face of the place; a daytime version with the shops shut also holds up, as long as the overhead layer is there)
- ② Key feature: the permanent layer overhead — dense clusters of paper lanterns in mixed white, red and yellow, varied in shape, tangled with vines, greenery and seasonal streamers — over an alley barely wide enough for two people to pass *(revised 2026-11-13 from the owner's real photos — field notes, correction 11; the first version of this card centered red lanterns and smoke, which staged the place)*
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: right next to Shinjuku Station's west exit. Tiny counter-only shops line the alley. Below the overhead layer, the mess has specific protagonists: stacks of red beer crates, round red stools, dense handwritten menu boards. People stand in small clusters rather than flowing through. The green entrance gate, white lettering with a trim of small bulbs, is the face of the place at night. The overhead lantern layer is present even in daytime, with the shops shut
- ④ Angle: not from the alley's entrance but from partway in, looking diagonally toward the back, the overhead layer always across the top of the frame. Muse stands toward the alley's edge
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: the faces of shopkeepers and customers → don't show them, or backs only / garbled pseudo-text on menu boards → keep them at a distance, rendered as scrawl / staged rows of identical red lanterns → the real layer is mixed colors and shapes, tangled with vines and decorations
- ⑥ Crowd density: medium (people standing in small clusters, and customers' backs at the counters)
- ⑦ Action restrictions: avoid compositions that photograph shop interiors or customers' faces (many shops refuse photography). Avoid standing poses that block the alley
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: the middle of a very narrow alley of tiny yakitori stalls in Omoide Yokocho, Nishi-Shinjuku, Tokyo
- KEY_FEATURE: a dense overhead layer of paper lanterns in mixed white, red and yellow, varied in shape, tangled with vines, greenery and seasonal streamers, above the narrow alley
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: the stalls are tiny counter-only shops; the alley is barely wide enough for two people to pass; red beer crates and round red stools stack at the shopfronts; people stand in small clusters; the overhead lantern layer is present even in daytime with the shops shut
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: from the middle of the alley looking along it at a slant, the overhead layer across the top of the frame; character pressed to one side
- PEOPLE: customers seen only from behind at the counters, small clusters of people standing and talking, blurred
- AVOID: no staged rows of identical red lanterns, no faces of staff or customers, no shop interior shot, no exotic street-stall grime

## 11. Don Quijote (Donki)

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience (the logo and mascot are D)
- Best time of day: night (being open until late at night is part of what this store is; the bright white interior lighting at night fits)
- ② Key feature: merchandise stacked up to the ceiling, and a flood of yellow and red POP cards
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: the aisles are narrow and maze-like, and merchandise is stacked nearly to the ceiling (compressed display). There are large numbers of handwritten-style yellow POP cards. The lighting is white and bright
- ④ Angle: inside a narrow aisle, looking slightly up at Muse, with shelves closing in on both sides. Don't use the store entrance or exterior
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: short shouted POP copy and giant price numbers → may render readable; correctly rendered text raises realism at this density (QA finding) / long text and product packaging names → blur, distance / garbled pseudo-text → reject the shot / the logo and penguin mascot → don't draw them
- ⑥ Crowd density: medium (customers' backs far down the aisle)
- ⑦ Action restrictions: don't draw the store's logo or mascot. Don't have Muse open or eat products. In-store photography is sometimes restricted by store rules
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: a narrow, maze-like aisle inside a Japanese discount variety store, Tokyo, late at night
- KEY_FEATURE: merchandise stacked densely up to the ceiling on both sides, signs and products hanging from the ceiling in slanted layers, a flood of bright yellow and red POP cards with giant price numbers and short shouted slogans
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: aisles are narrow and cramped; shelves are overfilled; the lighting is bright white fluorescent
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: low angle from inside the aisle, shelves closing in on both sides; no storefront
- PEOPLE: one or two shoppers' backs down the aisle, blurred
- AVOID: no store logo, no penguin mascot, no garbled pseudo-text on packaging, no opening products

## 12. An Ordinary Izakaya

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience
- Best time of day: night (the memory of an izakaya is the warm-colored lighting and liveliness at night; many are not open at midday)
- ② Key feature: on the table — a medium mug of draft beer, the small otoshi appetizer dish, and a hand towel
- ③ Facts that differ from the average image: once you sit down, an otoshi appetizer is served. Draft beer comes in a handled medium mug. The lighting is warm-colored. Seating is one of: tatami room, sunken table, or counter. Groups of customers around you make it noisy
- ④ Angle: at table height, from the opposite seat. Muse sits across the table, with the mug and the small dish in the foreground. Blur the walls
- ⑤ Breakable elements and handling: text on the wall's menu strips and lanterns → out of focus / other customers' faces → don't show them, or blur backs only / text on the chopstick wrapper → face it away
- ⑥ Crowd density: medium (a blurred presence of customers in the background)
- ⑦ Action restrictions: don't show other customers' faces. A drinking action draws a mouth, so have Muse only hold the mug with both hands or set it beside them
- SPOT_DESCRIPTION: a table in an ordinary, busy Japanese izakaya, {AREA}
- KEY_FEATURE: a handled mug of draft beer, a small dish of otoshi appetizer and a rolled wet hand towel on the table
- FACTS_AGAINST_STEREOTYPE: warm tungsten lighting; chopsticks in a paper sleeve and small plates on a slightly cluttered table; seating is tatami room, sunken table or counter
- ANGLE_AND_CROP: at table height from the opposite seat; the character seated across the table; the wall behind out of focus
- PEOPLE: other customers in the background, only backs and blurred shapes
- AVOID: no readable menu strips or lanterns, no customers' faces, no character drinking with an open mouth

## 15. Yamanote Line (Station Platform)

- ① Type: A — atmosphere/experience

| Item | Explanation |
|---|---|
